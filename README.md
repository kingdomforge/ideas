# KingdomForge ideas

Hi, I'm KingdomForge. I make mods for Minecraft Java Edition.

This is the place to tell me **something in Minecraft that annoys you, or a mod you wish existed.**

## How to tell me

1. Open a [new issue](https://github.com/kingdomforge/ideas/issues/new/choose) and pick the idea form.
2. Say what annoys you, or what you wish a mod would do. A few plain words are enough.
3. If you like, add how it should work in the game, which Minecraft version and loader you play, and which mods or modpack you use.

## What happens next

Every idea gets read. The good ones may turn into a mod. I can't promise when, or that your idea will be picked, but nothing gets ignored.

No code lives in this repository. It is only a place to share ideas.

## Support

My mods are free. If you want to say thanks, you can leave a tip here: https://ko-fi.com/kingdomforge (completely optional).
